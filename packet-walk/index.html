VLAN-backed VPC 拿掉了 NSX 的 Geneve。VPC subnet 就是 VLAN 8 上的一段 IP,路由在每台 ESX 的 DTGW 做完,只有 LoadBalancer VIP 要經過 VNA。圖上看到的 Geneve,是 Antrea 在節點之間自己包的。

A VLAN-backed VPC removes NSX Geneve. A VPC subnet is just a range of IPs on VLAN 8, routing finishes in the DTGW on every ESX host, and only LoadBalancer VIPs pass through the VNA. The Geneve you see in the diagram is Antrea's own tunnel between nodes.

    規則:VIP 一律拿 IP block 裡最小的可用單一 IP。NSX 沒有「VIP 專區」設定,只能把比 VIP 區小的可用 IP 先排除。開新 namespace 前,再把預留的 /24 解除排除。

    Rule: a VIP always takes the lowest free single IP in the IP block. NSX has no "VIP range" setting, so exclude every free address below the VIP range. Before you create a new namespace, release the /24 you held for it.

    • SubnetSet(Public、256 個 IP)依建立順序拿下一個 /24,不能指定 CIDR。
    • excluded_ips 建好後可以 PATCH,但要同時帶 cidrs 和 visibility。少了 visibility 會回 520051,訊息看起來像「不能改」。
    • visibility 和 subnet_exclusive 建好後不能改。
    • External IP block 要跟 Distributed VLAN 的閘道網段一致,否則回 640022。
    • 已經配出去的 VIP 不會因為改排除而變,刪掉 Service 重建才會換。
    • A SubnetSet (Public, 256 IPs) takes the next /24 in creation order. You cannot pick the CIDR.
    • excluded_ips can be PATCHed later, but the body must carry both cidrs and visibility. Without visibility NSX returns 520051, and the message reads like "cannot be changed".
    • visibility and subnet_exclusive cannot change after creation.
    • The External IP block must match the gateway network of the Distributed VLAN connection; otherwise NSX returns 640022.
    • Changing exclusions does not move a VIP that is already allocated. Delete and recreate the Service to get a new one.

    VNA 的容量VNA capacity

    Small → Supervisor 卡 CONFIGURINGSmall → Supervisor stuck in CONFIGURING

    VNA 至少選 MediumPick Medium or larger for the VNA

    Small 只放得下 1 個 LB。VKS 至少要 3 個(kube-system、supervisor-services、一個工作 namespace)。選 Small 時精靈照樣送出,Supervisor 一直停在 CONFIGURING,不會報錯(m01 實測 2026-11-14)。

    A Small VNA holds one load balancer. VKS needs at least three (kube-system, supervisor-services and one workload namespace). The wizard still accepts Small, and the Supervisor stays in CONFIGURING without an error (m01, 2026-11-14).

    20 VS · 60 pool · 400 member

    每個 namespace 一個 SMALL LBOne SMALL LB per namespace

    vCenter 自動建的 namespace VPC,LB 固定是 SMALL。要更大的 LB,得先建好 VPC 並指定 LB size,再讓 namespace 用那個 VPC。

    The VPC that vCenter creates for a namespace always gets a SMALL load balancer. For a bigger one, pre-create the VPC with an LB size and point the namespace at it.

    member = worker 數 × Service 數members = workers × Services

    每台 worker 都在 pool 裡Every worker is in every pool

    每個 LoadBalancer Service 都把全部 worker 的 NodePort 放進 pool,externalTrafficPolicy=Local 也一樣,只改健康檢查。worker 多的時候,member 上限會比 VS 上限先用完。

    Each LoadBalancer Service puts the NodePort of every worker into its pool, even with externalTrafficPolicy=Local, which only changes the health check. With many workers you hit the member limit before the VS limit.
